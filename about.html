About

Bridging the gap between open data and community insight.

The Mission: Data for the Community

San Antonio is growing rapidly, but the data that tracks our city's evolution—from transit reliability and housing trends to urban planning and resource equity—is often locked away in complex databases or scattered across various government portals.

CivicData SA exists to bridge that gap. This project takes raw, open data and transforms it into clear, visual, and actionable insights. Whether you are looking for a new place to live, advocating for better neighborhood services, or simply trying to understand how our city is changing, this site provides the information you need to stay informed and engaged.

The Methodology: Transparent & Open

Everything on this site is built with transparency in mind. Data is sourced from public records, including the U.S. Census Bureau, Open Data SA, and other local public portals.

  • For Residents: Analyses are designed to be digestible and relevant to everyday life in San Antonio.
  • For Builders: Every project is a "glass box." Each analysis includes a link to its GitHub repository with the Python code and methodologies used.

Behind the Project

CivicData SA is maintained by a local Data Analyst and UTSA graduate. This project started out of a personal interest in civic technology and a belief that data is most valuable when it's in the hands of the people it affects. I'm a relative newcomer to San Antonio, but I've come to love this city and want to understand it better. If this work helps it grow in the right direction, even a little, that's all I'm after.
