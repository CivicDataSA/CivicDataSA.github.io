Why this exists

Public data about our city should be as accessible as it is transparent. CivicData SA takes complex datasets—from housing trends to transit reliability—and turns them into neighborhood-level answers.

Whether you are looking for a new place to live, advocating for better services, or simply curious about how San Antonio is changing, this site provides the clear, actionable insights you need to stay informed and engaged.
